Why greedy decoding caps pass@k

At temperature 0 the model returns the same answer every time, so drawing k samples draws the same sample k times. The distinct column shows it directly: 1.0 distinct of {{ result.samples }} at T=0. pass@k cannot exceed pass@1 there, however large k gets.

Scored with 1 − C(n−c, k) / C(n, k) rather than "did any of my k samples pass", which is biased upward and moves with how many you happened to draw — which is why numbers from different papers are so often not comparable.
